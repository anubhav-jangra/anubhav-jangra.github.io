Looking for research internship opportunities for Summer 2027.

I am a fourth year CS Ph.D. student at Columbia University, where I work on developing and evaluating generative models for real-world applications. My research interests include diverse text generation, personalized text generation, text style evaluation, human-centered evaluation and AI for education. I'm fortunate to be advised by Smaranda Muresan.

In summer 2026, I interned as an Applied Science intern at the Microsoft Copilot Studio team, where I worked on skill evaluation for agents with Samarth Gupta and Deepak Sharma. In summer 2024, I have worked as an intern at Microsoft Research where I worked on style-personalized document generation for Word Copilot with Sujay Jauhar, Bahar Sarrafzadeh, Adrian de Wynter and Silviu Cucerzan. Before my PhD, I was working at Google Research India as a pre-doctoral researcher in the Advertising Sciences team with Aravindan Raghuveer and Preksha Nema on text style transfer for creative ad generation. I received my undergraduate degree from IIT Patna.
